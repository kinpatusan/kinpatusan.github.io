Frequently asked questions

A pattern collection I bought is still locked
Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Please check that you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.
I moved to a new iPhone and my marks are gone
In this version, your marks and your current row are saved only on the device. Before you switch, export a backup of your marks from Settings and keep the file in the Files app. On the new device, restore it from Settings.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds for in-app purchases are handled by Apple. Please request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
Where do the charts come from?
Every chart comes from a book or design dated 1925 or earlier, and we include only charts for which we have confirmed that the rights of everyone involved have expired. Each chart was redrawn square by square from images published by libraries and museums.
May I sell what I make?
Yes. You may use, give away, or sell what you make from these charts. Please do not redistribute screenshots of the app or the chart images.

About this app

A collection of knitting charts and embroidery patterns from pattern books and designs dated 1597 to 1925, redrawn as clear grids. You can mark finished stitches, keep your place row by row, and view each chart in the craft you work in. Your charts and your progress stay on your device.
