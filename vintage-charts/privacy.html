Privacy Policy

In short: this app does not collect your personal information. Your charts and your progress never leave your device.

Information we collect

  • There is no account to create, and the app never asks for your name or email address.
  • The app collects no data for analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • The developer runs no servers for this app.

What stays on your device

  • The marks you add, your current row, your gauge and display settings, and the craft you choose are saved only on your device.
  • All charts are included in the app. Opening a chart does not use the network.
  • The developer cannot see any of this. Deleting the app deletes these records.
  • If you export a backup of your marks, the file is saved where you choose (for example, in the Files app). The app does not send it anywhere.
  • This version does not sync with iCloud.

The App Store

  • The app communicates with Apple’s App Store only to show prices, to make purchases, and to restore purchases.
  • Payments are handled by Apple. Neither the app nor the developer receives your card number or other payment details.
  • Every pattern collection is a one-time purchase (non-consumable). There are no subscriptions.
  • Purchase history and refunds follow Apple’s policies (Apple Privacy Policy).

Links

  • The privacy policy and contact links in Settings simply open this website in your browser. The app sends no data when you open them.

Changes to this policy

  • If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date below.
  • If a future version handles information differently (for example, by adding iCloud sync), this page will be updated before that version is released.
