SupportPrivacy

Here is the conclusion first. This app collects no personal information from its users, and sends nothing to any external server.

Information We Collect

  • This app does not collect or transmit users' personal information.
  • There is no account registration.
  • No data is collected for usage analytics or for advertising.

Location Data

  • Location is used only to show your current position on the map and to let you see where you are in relation to candidate sites.
  • Location data is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted or stored elsewhere.
  • You can turn it off at any time in your device settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services).

The Records Feature

  • Records such as "Want to go", "Been there" and "Found it" are stored only on your device.
  • The developer has no way of seeing these records.

In-App Purchases

  • In-app purchases are processed through Apple's App Store.
  • Neither this app nor its developer ever receives payment details such as credit card numbers.
  • All purchases are one-time (non-consumable); there are no subscriptions and no automatic renewals.
  • Purchase history and refunds are handled according to Apple's policy.

Reporting a Find by Email

  • "Report a find or visit" simply opens the mail app on your own device.
  • Whether to send anything, and what to write, is entirely up to you. The app never sends anything automatically.
  • Anything you do send is used solely to verify and improve the data in the app.

External Services

  • Apple's MapKit is used to display the map background.
  • The handling of data when map tiles and the like are retrieved is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Contact

  • For questions about this policy, please use the contact address given on the support page.

Revisions

  • If this policy is revised, this page will be updated and the last-updated date changed.
